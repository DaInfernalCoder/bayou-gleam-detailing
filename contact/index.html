What to share

  • Vehicle make, model, size, and the main areas needing attention.
  • Visible dirt, pet hair, stains, odors, damage, films, coatings, or prior treatment.
  • Whether you want interior work, exterior work, a wash, headlight polishing, or a combination.
  • The proposed location, parking permission, access, and any water, power, or runoff restrictions.
  • When the vehicle will be available and when it must return to use.

Before the appointment is confirmed

Ask for the included work and quote, any add-ons being considered, and the preparation needed at the location. If shampooing or another moisture-based interior treatment is proposed, discuss drying expectations. If a mark, lens condition, or damaged surface may require a repair rather than cleaning, raise that before booking.

For a workplace, apartment, garage, or managed property, confirm permission and restrictions with the property first. Tell the business about gates, clearance, parking rules, and utility availability. The actual location and vehicle condition help determine what is practical.
