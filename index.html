04 · Before you schedule

Useful answers, up front.

If your vehicle has a specific stain, odor, paint issue, or access concern, mention it when you call or book online.

How do I get started?

Call 346-509-3342 or book online. Share the vehicle, location, and what you want addressed.

Do I need to bring the car somewhere?

No. Bayou Gleam is a mobile service. The work is scheduled where the vehicle is parked at your Houston home or workplace.

How is the right service chosen?

We use the vehicle size, current condition, and your priorities to determine whether an interior, exterior, or full detail fits best.

Can I ask about one specific problem?

Yes. Describe the issue when you call or book. Photos can help explain stains, heavy buildup, or a paint concern before the visit.

Are the photos on this site Bayou Gleam customer vehicles?

No. They are licensed stock photos used to show detailing subjects and surfaces. They are not presented as Bayou Gleam's completed work.
